# Mini Buddy 🐾

> A delightful, lightweight macOS desktop companion and interactive billiard physics screensaver.

## ✨ Features

- **🐾 Desktop Companion**: Floating quietly on your desktop without cluttering your workspace, naturally looking at your mouse cursor in real-time.
- **👀 Real-Time Gaze Tracking**: Interactive 3D vector-projected eyes follow the pointer with natural micro-saccades and fluid head movement.
- **✨ Dynamic Moods & Expressions**: Joyful, surprised, sleepy, curious, and playful moods switch dynamically.
- **🎱 High-Speed Billiard Physics Screensaver**: Launches your buddy with aerodynamic speed-wake trails, transferring kinetic collision momentum to the date & time widget with 4-border cushion bouncing.
- **🔒 Privacy First**: 100% offline, zero tracking, and lightweight footprint.

---

## 🔒 Privacy Policy

Mini Buddy is designed with privacy as the highest priority:
- **Zero Data Collection**: We do NOT collect, store, track, or share any personal data or usage analytics.
- **100% Offline**: All rendering, physics simulations, and interactions run entirely locally on your Mac.
